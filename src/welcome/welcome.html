CleanThis is in.

Clean files of hidden threats and check webpages for scams — processed by cleanthis.io, controlled entirely by you.

Tip: pin CleanThis to the toolbar (puzzle-piece icon → 📌) so a scan is always one click away.

Scan any page — ready now

Click the toolbar icon for a safety verdict on the page you're on — security, privacy and legitimacy scores with the same reasons the website's report shows. Or right-click any link and choose Scan with CleanThis. Nothing is ever scanned without your click.

Clean risky downloads

Off by default. While it is on, the web address of a matching download is sent to cleanthis.io, which fetches and cleans the file and gives you the cleaned copy. You can always take the original instead — nothing is ever blocked outright. Which file types count is a set of checkboxes in settings.

Flagged-site warnings

Off by default. Checked on your device against a local copy of cleanthis.io's flagged-site list — the addresses you visit never leave your machine, and no site is ever blocked outright. Turning it on asks for the browser's "tabs" and "webNavigation" permissions in one prompt.

Private by wiring, not by promise

Nothing is sent unless you ask for it. The extension talks only to cleanthis.io, collects no analytics, and keeps no history of what you visit or clean.
